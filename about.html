About Us

The story behind our passion for baking

Our Story

Karina's Bakery began as a small dream in a tiny kitchen, fueled by a passion for creating delicious treats that bring people together. What started as baking cookies for neighbors and friends has grown into a beloved community bakery that serves hundreds of happy customers every week.

Our founder, Karina, learned the art of baking from her grandmother, who taught her that the secret ingredient in every recipe is love. That philosophy continues to guide us today as we carefully select the finest ingredients and time-honored recipes to create treats that are truly special.

Meet Karina

👩‍🍳

Karina has been baking professionally for over 15 years. After culinary school and apprenticeships at renowned bakeries across Europe, she returned home to open her dream bakery.

"When I see the smiles on people's faces when they taste something I've made, I know I'm exactly where I'm meant to be. Baking isn't just my profession— it's my way of spreading joy."

— Karina, Founder & Head Baker
